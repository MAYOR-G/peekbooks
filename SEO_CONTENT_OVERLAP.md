## Regional overlap: UK and USA

Both pages have self-referencing canonicals, separate regional spelling/service copy and early search impressions. They are retained. Reciprocal `en-GB`/`en-US` hreflang is appropriate only if the owner confirms both regional operations and keeps the pages genuinely equivalent. LocalBusiness schema is deferred until office staffing and business records are verified. No East Lansing, Glasgow, state or city landing pages will be created.

## Deliberate non-consolidation decisions

- Preserve all current URLs in Groups A–D.
- No redirects are added merely to reduce shared vocabulary.
- No article is converted into a transactional landing page.
- No publication date is changed without a substantive edit recorded in the changelog.
- No category/tag archives are created as indexable duplicate URLs.
