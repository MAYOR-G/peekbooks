## Cannibalisation rules

1. Service pages answer “hire/submit/what is included”; articles answer “learn/compare/plan/check.”
2. `/thesis-editing` owns thesis editing transactions. The comparison article must link to it but must not replace it.
3. `/journal-paper-editing` owns journal-manuscript editing; `/manuscript-editing` is narrowed to books and professional long-form documents.
4. `/academic-editing` owns broad research-paper editing but not thesis or target-journal-specific requests.
5. The academic cost article owns thesis/dissertation/research factors. The general cost article uses business, author and professional examples.
6. The UK/USA pages remain separate only while they provide genuine regional value. No city/state pages will be created.
7. Existing URLs with promising average positions are not renamed or redirected without a verified migration reason.
