# Peekbooks SEO Backlink & GSC Action Plan

This document outlines the sustainable strategy for building domain authority, managing indexation via Google Search Console (GSC), and promoting the newly created blog content.

## Phase 1: Google Search Console (GSC) Setup & Indexation

**1. Claim and Verify GSC Property**
- Ensure the property `https://www.peekbookeditors.com` is verified in Google Search Console using the HTML file method or DNS record.
- Upload the `sitemap.xml` directly to GSC under the "Sitemaps" tab (`https://www.peekbookeditors.com/sitemap.xml`).

**2. Request Manual Indexing for New Blogs**
- Now that the `noindex` tags have been removed from the blog detail pages, submit the three new blog URLs manually using the GSC "URL Inspection" tool:
  - `/blog/dissertation-proofreading-checklist`
  - `/blog/proofreading-costs-guide`
  - `/blog/proofreading-vs-editing`
- Also, inspect the `/blog` index page and request indexing.

**3. Monitor Coverage**
- Check the "Pages" report weekly to ensure no valid pages are marked as "Crawled - currently not indexed" or "Discovered - currently not indexed."

## Phase 2: Sustainable Backlink Strategy

Building authority in the academic and professional editing niche requires high-trust links. Avoid PBNs (Private Blog Networks) or paid link farms. 

### Strategy 1: Academic Outreach & Resource Pages
- **Target:** University writing centers, graduate student resource pages, and departmental blogs.
- **Action:** Pitch the "Ultimate Dissertation Proofreading Checklist" as a free resource for their students.
- **Email Pitch Example:** "Hi [Name], I noticed you have a great resource page for grad students writing their dissertations. We recently published a comprehensive, free proofreading checklist that might be a helpful addition..."

### Strategy 2: LinkedIn & Professional Networks
- **Target:** PhD candidates, independent authors, and business professionals on LinkedIn.
- **Action:** Publish articles on LinkedIn Pulse summarizing the "Proofreading vs. Editing" guide, and link back to the full article on the Peekbooks site.
- **Action:** Share the "How Much Does Proofreading Cost?" guide in writer groups or academic publishing forums where members frequently ask about pricing.

### Strategy 3: Google Business Profile (GBP) Posts
- **Target:** Local and regional clients searching for editing services.
- **Action:** Since Peekbooks has physical office locations, utilize the Google Business Profile. Post weekly updates linking directly to the new blog posts.
- **Benefit:** GBP posts send strong local SEO signals and drive immediate, high-intent traffic.

### Strategy 4: Guest Posting on Author/Academic Blogs
- **Target:** Blogs focused on self-publishing (e.g., Jane Friedman, The Creative Penn) or academic survival (e.g., The Thesis Whisperer).
- **Action:** Pitch unique, high-quality guest posts related to manuscript preparation, and include a natural, contextual link back to Peekbooks' services or the "Proofreading vs. Editing" guide.
